Anderson Home Automation

Anderson Home Automation is a private, self-hosted smart-home application run by Garet Anderson for his own household. It connects the household's Google accounts and devices to a home automation server that runs on hardware inside the home. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What the app does

  • Thermostat control. Reads the status of the household's Google Nest thermostats (temperature, humidity, mode, and set points) and changes their settings when a household member asks, for example from a dashboard, a schedule, or a voice command.
  • Thermostat events. Receives notifications from Google when a thermostat changes, so the home server shows current values without constant polling.
  • Google Assistant commands. Sends text commands and spoken announcements to the household's own Google Assistant on request, for example to broadcast a reminder in the home.

Why it asks for access to your Google account

When a household member signs in with Google, the app asks for these permissions, and uses each one only for the feature listed:

Permission (scope)Used for
Google Nest Smart Device Management
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/sdm.service		 Reading and changing the household's Nest thermostat settings.
Google Cloud Pub/Sub
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/pubsub		 Receiving thermostat change notifications from Google.
Google Assistant SDK
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/assistant-sdk-prototype		 Sending the household's own commands and announcements to Google Assistant.

The app does not read email, contacts, files, photos, calendar, or location from your Google account, does not sell or share Google user data, and does not use it for advertising. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

Only Google accounts belonging to the owner's household, which the owner has added as users. Anyone else who reaches the sign-in screen cannot use the app.
